# MedLens design system

The user-provided MedLens design v2 is the visual authority. Operate mode: evidence and tasks precede generated prose. Use a white and cool near-white canvas, one blue accent, compact typography, aligned tabular numerals and thin borders. A permanent navigation rail frames the workspace. Status badges always pair an icon with source-relative language.

Primary actions are 44px rounded buttons; inputs use 8px corners; panels use 12px corners. Source review uses a two-pane document and extraction layout. Motion is limited to state changes and respects reduced motion. No decorative imagery, health scores, gradients, or chat-first design.

Canonical terminology lives in lib/clinicalTerms.ts. Confidence is qualitative and justified rather than an invented percentage. Template summaries are labeled as rule-based instead of AI generated.
