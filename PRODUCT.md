# MedLens

A clinical information workspace for people organizing patient intake and reports before human review. Upload or paste report information, inspect structured lab results beside the exact source, correct and verify them, resolve conflicting records, and export a traceable record.

Product requirements come from the two user-provided briefs. The app does not diagnose or give treatment advice. Source ranges are never inferred. Zero-key deterministic processing is required. A clearly fictional demo makes the workflow immediately reviewable. Records are ephemeral and browser-session scoped by default.

Assumptions: a single local workspace, no preexisting identity provider, English extraction, and a desktop-first responsive web app. No cloud deployment is requested.
